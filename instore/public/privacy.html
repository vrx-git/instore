INSTORE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

INSTORE is an inventory app for shops. This page explains what data INSTORE handles and where it is kept.

Data stored on your device

Your items, stock movements, stores, categories and staff profiles (names and 4-digit PIN hashes) are stored in your browser on your own device. INSTORE does not send this data to any server run by us.

Google backup (optional)

If you turn on Backup email in Settings and sign in with Google:

  • INSTORE creates a Google Sheet called "INSTORE Data" in your own Google Drive and keeps it in sync with your devices.
  • INSTORE asks Google only for permission to see the files it creates (drive.file) and your email address (to confirm the right account is used). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept only in your browser for about an hour and is never sent to us.
  • The data goes directly between your device and Google. We do not receive, store, sell or share it.

INSTORE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • To stop syncing, use Settings → Backup email → Stop backup.
  • To remove INSTORE's access to Google, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your data, delete the "INSTORE Data" sheet from your Google Drive and remove the app from your device.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes on your device. No images are stored or sent anywhere.
